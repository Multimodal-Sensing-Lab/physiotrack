AFLW angles converted from radians to degrees
AFLW angle signs inverted to match the PhysioTrack output convention

Evaluation protocol:
AFLW provides approximate coarse head-pose annotations derived from facial landmarks using POSIT. The annotations are not manually verified for every face.
The primary evaluation therefore reports controlled coarse-pose accuracy rather than fine-grained ground-truth pose accuracy.
AFLW ground-truth face rectangles were used to isolate head-pose estimation from face detection.
Primary pose range: |yaw| <= 90 degrees, |pitch| < 90 degrees, |roll| <= 90 degrees
The controlled range avoids Euler-angle boundary and extreme-orientation cases where axis-wise angle comparison becomes ambiguous.
Angular errors use wrapped absolute angular difference in degrees.
