Annotation format: 68-point PTS files

Protocol:
51-point landmark evaluation
17 face-border landmarks excluded
Normalization: inter-ocular distance between outer eye corners
Prediction: PhysioTrack FaceLandmarks.predict_face()
Model: MediaPipe Face Landmarker (478 landmarks)
Evaluation adapter: fixed 51-point MediaPipe-to-300-W anatomical mapping
Face initialization: GT-derived padded bounding box from the 68-point annotation
Padding: 20 percent of GT landmark bounding-box width and height
Purpose: isolate landmark localization from face detection and face selection
The results are controlled component validation values, not official 300-W competition leaderboard results.
